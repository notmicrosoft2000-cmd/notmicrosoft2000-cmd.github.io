PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Overview

Neptune Inc. (the "Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at notmicrosoft2000-cmd.github.io (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you visit the Site. We are a fictional, publicly traded company, allegedly. This Privacy Policy is not fictional.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers. The Site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no backend server, no database, and no analytics service that transmits your data to us.

3. Information Stored Locally

The Site uses your browser's localStorage to persist certain preferences and state. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. The stored items include:

  • Theme preference \u2014 the visual theme you selected.
  • Simple mode \u2014 whether you enabled or disabled the simplified view.
  • Reduced motion \u2014 whether you disabled animations.
  • Sound settings \u2014 whether sound effects are enabled.
  • Project matching \u2014 whether the site restyles to match the active project.
  • Recently visited \u2014 a list of up to 4 project keys you last visited.
  • GitHub cache \u2014 cached GitHub API responses for project stats (expires after 15 minutes).
  • Release seen tags \u2014 the last seen release tag for each project (used for update notifications).
  • Intro/notification dismissed \u2014 whether you dismissed the welcome toast or navigation hint.
  • Forum username \u2014 if you entered a username in the public forum.
  • Forum messages \u2014 the last 80 messages received in the public forum (stored in memory only, not persisted).

You can clear all of this data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site, or by using your browser's developer tools.

4. Third-Party Services

GitHub API: When you visit a project page, the Site makes requests to the GitHub API (api.github.com) to fetch repository statistics and release information. These requests are made directly from your browser and are subject to GitHub's privacy policy. We cache the responses in localStorage for 15 minutes to reduce API calls.

MQTT Broker: The public forum connects to a third-party MQTT broker (broker.emqx.io) to send and receive messages. Messages are sent in plaintext and are visible to anyone subscribed to the topic. Do not send sensitive information through the forum. The forum broker is operated by EMQ and is subject to their terms.

Google Fonts: The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When you load the Site, Google may receive your IP address and browser information. Google's privacy policy applies.

5. Cookies

The Site does not use cookies.

6. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the Site's contact page or open an issue on the GitHub repository.
