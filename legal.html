The Company does not collect, transmit, store, process, aggregate, monetize, sell, barter, trade, lend, lease, sublease, or otherwise obtain any personal information on any server, database, cloud instance, punch card, or other data storage mechanism. 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Site utilizes your browser's localStorage API to persist certain preferences and state. This data resides exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us, to GitHub, to any third party, or to any celestial body. The Company has compiled the following exhaustive (and, the Company admits, somewhat tedious) list of stored items:

  • Theme preference — The visual theme you selected. The Company is pleased you made a choice.
  • Simple mode — Whether you enabled or disabled the simplified view. No judgment either way.
  • Reduced motion — Whether you disabled animations. The Company respects your vestibular system.
  • Sound settings — Whether sound effects are enabled. The beeps are optional, but the Company is fond of them.
  • Project matching — Whether the site restyles to match the active project. This is, the Company admits, a purely aesthetic decision with no functional consequence whatsoever.
  • Recently visited — A list of up to 4 project keys you last visited. The Company does not judge your browsing habits.
  • GitHub cache — Cached GitHub API responses for project statistics. These expire after 15 minutes, at which point the Company re-fetches them, whether you asked it to or not.
  • Release seen tags — The last seen release tag for each project, used for update notifications. The Company is very proud of its update notifications.
  • Intro/notification dismissed — Whether you dismissed the welcome toast or navigation hint. If you dismissed it, the Company will not show it again. The Company respects your decision, however misguided.
  • Forum username — If you entered a username in the public forum. The Company hopes you chose wisely.
  • Legal consent — Whether you agreed to the legal documents. The Company is legally required to store this. The Company is also legally required to inform you that it is not legally required to do anything, being fictional.

You may clear all of this data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for this Site, or by using your browser's developer tools, or by performing a factory reset on your device, which the Company does not recommend but will not stop you from doing.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site interfaces with certain third-party services. The Company is contractually (allegedly) obligated to inform you of this:

4.1 GitHub API

When you visit a project page, the Site makes requests to the GitHub API (api.github.com) to fetch repository statistics and release information. These requests are made directly from your browser and are subject to GitHub's Privacy Statement. The Company caches the responses in localStorage for 15 minutes to reduce API calls, which the Company feels is a reasonable and neighborly thing to do.

4.2 MQTT Broker

The public forum connects to a third-party MQTT broker (broker.emqx.io) to send and receive messages. Messages are transmitted in plaintext and are visible to anyone subscribed to the topic. The Company strongly advises against sending sensitive information through the forum. The Company also strongly advises against sending insensitive information through the forum, but this is a free country (allegedly). The forum broker is operated by EMQ Technologies Co., Ltd. and is subject to EMQ's Terms of Service.

4.3 Google Fonts

The Site loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When you load the Site, Google may receive your IP address, browser information, and a reasonable approximation of your taste in typography. Google's privacy policy applies, and the Company suggests you take it up with Google if you have concerns, because the Company certainly will not.

The Company has no power over your localStorage and would not exercise such power even if it could, on principle.
